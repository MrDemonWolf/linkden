# Welcome to LinkDen

Create a fast, beautiful link-in-bio page that you fully own and control. LinkDen runs on your own infrastructure, gives you a polished admin panel, and loads instantly for visitors worldwide.

**Your links, your brand, your rules.**

### ⚡ TL;DR
LinkDen is a self-hosted alternative to Linktree.
- 🚀 **Fast:** Runs on Cloudflare Edge.
- 🎨 **Beautiful:** 11 themes, dark mode, custom banners.
- 📱 **Mobile-First:** Apple Wallet integration, vCard support.
- 🔒 **Private:** You own the data.

## Why LinkDen?

- ⚡ **Blazing fast** -- Pages load in under a second, served from the edge.
- 🎛️ **Full admin panel** -- Manage blocks, appearance, analytics, vCard, and Apple Wallet passes from one dashboard.
- 🎨 **11 built-in themes** -- Each with dark and light variants plus auto mode.
- 🧱 **9 block types** -- Links, headers, embeds, connect (contact form), vCard, location, image, text, and divider.
- 🌐 **50+ social brands** -- Searchable icon library with automatic dark-mode support.
- 🖼️ **Banner system** -- Pick from preset gradients or upload your own image.
- 🍏 **Apple Wallet** -- Generate a signed `.pkpass` business card to share from your iPhone.
- 🏷️ **Whitelabel** -- Remove all LinkDen branding with a single toggle. MIT licensed.
- 💾 **Export/Import** -- Back up and restore all your data as JSON.
- 🛡️ **Secure by default** -- Two-factor sign-in, rate limiting, XSS protection, URL validation, CSS sanitization, file upload restrictions, and a single-admin lock enforced in the database.
