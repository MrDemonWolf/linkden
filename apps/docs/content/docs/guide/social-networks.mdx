title: Social Networks
description: Add social media links with brand icons that show under your profile.
---

LinkDen includes 50+ social network icons based on [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/). Manage them at `/admin/links/social`; active networks appear as a row of icons under your profile on the public page (there is no separate block to add).

## Finding and Activating Networks

### Search

Use the search box under **Add Social Network** to find an available service by name.

### Add a Network

Select a service under **Add Social Network**. Enter its full profile URL in the dialog, including `https://` or `http://`, then choose **Add Network**. Choose **Save changes** in the sticky save bar to save your edits.

Each available service shows its category below its name.

### Toggle and URL

Existing networks have a toggle switch and a username/URL field. Enter a URL before enabling a network. For services with a profile template, you can edit the row using a handle, an `@handle`, or a full profile URL; LinkDen stores a clean link. Switching a network off keeps it in your list; use the trash button to remove it. Choose **Save changes** to save your changes, or **Discard** to restore the saved list.

> **Tip:** Dark brand icons are automatically inverted in dark mode so they stay visible regardless of your theme.

The preview shows unsaved social edits. Save your changes, then publish a new revision to update an assets-only public page. Switching a network off keeps its saved URL; only the trash action removes it.

## How They Appear

Active networks show as icons under your name and bio. Choose **circle** or **rounded square** icons under Design → Theme (**Social icon shape**). Style them further with Custom CSS (`.ld-social-block`).
