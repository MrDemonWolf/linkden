# Social Networks

LinkDen includes 50+ social network icons based on [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/). Manage them at `/admin/links/social`; active networks appear as a row of icons under your profile on the public page (there is no separate block to add).

## Finding and Activating Networks

### Search

Use the search box to quickly find any network by name.

### Category Tabs

Filter networks by category:

- **Social Media**
- **Messaging**
- **Developer**
- **Business**
- **Content**
- **Music & Audio**
- **Gaming**

### Toggle and URL

Each network has a toggle switch and a username/URL field. Turn a network on, then enter your handle, an `@handle`, or paste your full profile URL (with or without `https://` or `www.`); LinkDen stores a clean link. Switching a network off keeps it in your list; use the trash button to remove it. Changes are saved with the save bar.

> **Tip:** Dark brand icons are automatically inverted in dark mode so they stay visible regardless of your theme.

## How They Appear

Active networks show as icons under your name and bio. Choose **circle** or **rounded square** icons under Design → Theme (**Social icon shape**). Style them further with Custom CSS (`.ld-social-block`).
