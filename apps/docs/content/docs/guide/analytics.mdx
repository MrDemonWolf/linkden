# Analytics

See how your page is performing at `/admin/insights`. LinkDen tracks visitor activity without relying on third-party services -- all data stays in your own database.

## Dashboard Overview

### Time Period

Filter all data by **7 days** (default), **30 days**, **90 days**, or **All time**. Changing the period updates every chart and stat on the page. Days are counted in the timezone set under **Settings → Account** (UTC when unset).

### Stat Cards

Two cards at the top give you the big picture:

- **Total Views** -- Page views for the selected period.
- **Total Clicks** -- Link clicks for the selected period.

### Views vs Clicks

A chart of daily page views and link clicks (Clicks is the dashed line). Every day of the period is shown, with zero on days without traffic. Hover a day for exact counts; screen readers get a table of the daily numbers.

### Top Links, Referrers, and Countries

Three cards below the chart break down your traffic:

- **Top Links** -- Your most-clicked links ranked by click count (top 10).
- **Referrers** -- Where your visitors are coming from. Direct visits (no referrer) show as "Direct" (top 10).
- **Countries** -- Which countries your visitors are in (top 20).

## What Gets Tracked

**Page views** record the timestamp, the referring site's hostname (for example `instagram.com`, never the full URL), and the visitor's country.

**Link clicks** record which link was clicked, the referring hostname, and the visitor's country.

Raw rows are kept for **365 days** and then deleted by the daily retention job.

## Privacy

LinkDen's analytics are designed to respect your visitors' privacy:

- **Consent first (by default):** with the consent banner on, nothing is recorded until the visitor accepts analytics. If you turn the banner off, every visit is recorded without asking. See [Consent Banner](/docs/guide/appearance#-consent-banner).
- No tracking cookies. The visitor's choice is kept in their browser's local storage.
- No IP addresses or user-agent strings are stored. Country comes from Cloudflare's request header.
- LinkDen adds no tracking scripts. Embed blocks and CAPTCHA load third-party content (see the [privacy page](/docs/privacy-policy)).
- No fingerprinting techniques are used.
- Analytics data lives in your own database and is never sent to an external service.
