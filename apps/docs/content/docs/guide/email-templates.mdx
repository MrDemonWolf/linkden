This page previews LinkDen's contact notification template, built with React Email. Authentication messages such as password reset are separate from this notification preview.

## Contact Notification

When a visitor submits your contact form, you receive a **Contact Notification** email with the full submission details:

- Sender name and email address
- Message content
- Optional fields: **Where we met** (when the form asks it) and **Form** (the Connect block's title)

The email includes a LinkDen header and a footer linking to the project. Sends time out after 10 seconds; a failure is logged without your API key and the submission is kept in the Inbox. The preview below does not send mail or verify your provider configuration.

## Setting Up Email Delivery

To receive contact form emails, configure an email provider in **Settings > Email**:

1. Configure **Resend**, the supported email provider.
2. Enter your API key.
3. Set your "From" email address (must be verified with the provider).
4. Under **Contact-form delivery**, choose **Email only** or **Inbox and email**.

See the [Contact Form guide](/docs/guide/contact-form) for full setup instructions.
