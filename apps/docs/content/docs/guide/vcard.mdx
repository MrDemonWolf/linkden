# vCard

Give visitors a one-tap way to save your contact info. When enabled, a **Save vCard** button appears in your public page's footer and downloads a `.vcf` file compatible with iOS, Android, macOS, and Windows. You can also add a **vCard block** with its own details anywhere on the page.

## Enabling vCard

1. Go to `/admin/design/branding`.
2. Find the **vCard** section.
3. Toggle it on.

The footer button downloads the first visible vCard block's card, or the details from this section when the page has no vCard block.

## Filling In Your Details

Edit your vCard fields in the vCard section of Design → Branding. Only fields you fill in are included in the downloaded file -- everything else is left out automatically. Phone numbers, work phone and birthday are checked as you type.

### Personal Information

- **Full Name** -- Your name as it should appear in contacts.
- **Nickname** -- An alternative name or handle.
- **Birthday** -- Your birthday.
- **Photo** -- Profile photo URL.

### Professional Information

- **Organization** -- Company or organization name.
- **Title** -- Your role or position.
- **Department** -- Department within the organization.

### Contact Details

- **Email** -- Personal email address.
- **Work Email** -- Work email address.
- **Phone** -- Personal phone number.
- **Work Phone** -- Work phone number.

### Address

Your full mailing address.

### URLs

Add links to your websites, social profiles, or any other URLs. Each entry has a label and a URL.

## Format

LinkDen generates vCard 3.0 files, which have the widest compatibility across devices and apps. Values are escaped (no line breaks can inject extra fields), the file always has a structured name (`N`), and the department is written as part of the organization.
